## 1. Context

Task p4.4 (phase-p4-security) adds a per-scheme allowlist to the handling of links to other apps
(`LaunchingExternalUriScheme`, adr-0015 rule 1: non-web schemes need explicit confirmation). Since P2 every such link
is offered on the shell's confirmation bar and nothing opens without the user's click.

## 2. The question (registered as Q-224)

"Next task: links that open another app (for example an email link that opens your mail program). Today Ferrum always
asks before opening one, and nothing opens without your click. The plan adds a list of allowed link types. What should
that list allow by default?"

Options offered:
- "Any type, always ask (Recommended)": "Every link type may be offered, always with a click. You can narrow it to a
  list in Settings. Claude also proposes a short built-in list of known-risky link types that are always refused (with
  sources, for your review)."
- "Common types only": "Only email (mailto:) and phone (tel:) links are offered; every other type is refused unless you
  add it in Settings."
- "None until you add": "Every link to another app is refused until you add its type in Settings."

## 3. The owner's answer (verbatim)

> Any type, always ask (Recommended)

## 4. Meaning recorded by Claude

Default: every non-web link type may be offered on the confirmation bar, and opens only after the user's click. A
settings list can narrow the offered types. Claude proposes a short built-in list of known-risky link types that are
always refused; that list is Claude's proposal, each entry with a source note, and it needs the owner's review
(it is not approved by this answer).
