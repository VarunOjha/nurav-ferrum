## Correction by Claude (2026-10-10)

The "Newest first" option said questions sort by question number because numbers go up in the order asked.
That is not true: question numbers come in topic series (Q-0xx, Q-1xx, Q-2xx), so Q-125 is newer than Q-221.
Claude told the owner in the next message; questions sort by the date asked, and same-day ties use their order on the questions page.
