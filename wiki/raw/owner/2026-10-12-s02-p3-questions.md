## 1. Context

The owner's directive ("consider auto approved for next phase") covers approvals, not open questions. Three open questions
block the P3 entry gate: Q-125 (title bar), Q-111 (profile granularity), Q-209 (cross-site navigation in per-site-profile
mode). Claude asked them in plain words with a recommended option each.

## 2. Questions and the owner's answers (verbatim)

1. Q-125 — "Tabs: where should they sit at first?"
   Options: "Normal title bar first (Recommended)"; "Tabs in the top bar now".
   > Normal title bar first (Recommended)

2. Q-111 — "Separate cookie stores ('profiles'): what counts as one site?"
   Options: "Main domain (Recommended)" (mail.example.com and docs.example.com share one profile, example.com);
   "Exact address" (every subdomain gets its own profile).
   > Main domain (Recommended)

3. Q-209 — "A tab for site A follows a link to a different site B. What should Ferrum do?"
   Options: "Switch to B's profile (Recommended)" (keeps sites separated; that tab's back button can't return to site A;
   Ferrum says so); "Stay in A's profile"; "Block it".
   > Can we have pop up to confirm, and then do 1

## 3. Meaning recorded by Claude

- Q-125 answered: option (a), the normal Windows title bar first, with the tab strip just under it.
- Q-111 answered: the profile key is the registrable domain (eTLD+1, "main domain"). Users merging sites into named
  containers (adr-0010's suggestion) is not part of the answer and is not built in P3.
- Q-209 answered: Ferrum first asks the user to confirm, then re-hosts the tab in site B's profile (option (a), with the
  loss of that tab's back history shown). "Pop up" is built as Ferrum's confirmation bar in the shell band (like the bar for
  links to other apps), because nothing may overlap the tab's web view (Tauri issue 15682). Cancel keeps the tab on site A.
