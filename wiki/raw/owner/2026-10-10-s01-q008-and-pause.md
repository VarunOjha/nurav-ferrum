## 1. Q-008: keep or remove the accessibility checker

Context: Q-008 asked whether the portal's browser tests may use the accessibility checker
`@axe-core/playwright` (a test-only dev dependency of `interactive_plan/`). Claude had already added the
checker on 2026-10-09 before the owner answered, as its engine `axe-core` 4.14.0, an exact devDependency (see
the correction note on Q-008 in `wiki/overview/ov-open-questions.md`).

Exchange (summary): the owner first asked what the checker does and why it is worth keeping. Claude explained:
it checks every portal tab automatically for colour contrast, popup behaviour (focus moves into a popup, Esc
closes it, focus returns to where it was), labels on buttons and links, and page structure (headings,
landmarks); it is test-only and never ships in the portal; its cost is small (one dev dependency and a few
seconds of test time); it cannot judge whether the wording is clear, which stays a human review.

Owner's answer (verbatim):

> Okay, lets add.

Meaning, as recorded: **Q-008 answered — KEEP the accessibility checker** (`@axe-core/playwright` / its engine
`axe-core`, test-only dev dependency of `interactive_plan/`).
